Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026.

Harbord Home uses Google's Smart Device Management API to read status from, and send commands to, the Nest devices of the single household that operates it. The only Google account it is authorized against belongs to that household.

Data accessed

Device information exposed by the Smart Device Management API: thermostat settings and readings, camera and doorbell events, and device names and structure.

Use and storage

Data is used only to display and automate those devices within the household's own Home Assistant server, which is hosted on its private network. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.
